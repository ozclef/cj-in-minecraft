# CJ in Minecraft

![CJ from GTA San Andreas in a Minecraft forest](docs/images/cover.jpg)

Carl Johnson from **GTA San Andreas** plays **Minecraft**. GTA runs beside Minecraft and gives CJ his animations, weapons, car and clothes; Minecraft
draws him in your world, and what you do in Minecraft moves him. **Nothing of GTA is included**: the installer converts CJ from your own copy of the
game.

## How to install Tutorial
[Here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eZYk-9uRG4E) you can see the process to install the mod.

## Features

- **CJ himself** in place of the Minecraft player, moved by GTA's own animations, dressed and built like CJ in your save.
- **GTA's weapons**: Colt 45, Desert Eagle, Micro SMG, shotgun, AK-47, M4, minigun and rocket launcher, with ammo, crafting recipes, durability,
  repair and chest loot; twin pistols at top skill; the baseball bat and CJ's fists.
- **Moving like CJ**: crouching, swimming and climbing as in GTA.
- **The Greenwood**: drive GTA's car through your Minecraft world (G key), with radio, headlights and fuel.
- **The jetpack**, with its own fuel.
- **First person**: CJ's arms and the weapon seen from his eyes, and driving from the driver's seat.
- **Skateboarding (optional)**: CJ skates with the physics, tricks and board of **Skate 3**, through the Skate 3 Rust engine and your own copy of
  Skate 3, with a gamepad.

## Requirements

- GTA San Andreas **1.0 US** with an **ASI loader** and **CLEO**.
- Minecraft Java **1.21.1** with **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API**.
- **Python 3.10** or newer (for the installer).
- On Linux, **Wine** for GTA.
- For skating: your **Skate 3 for Xbox 360** and a **gamepad**.
